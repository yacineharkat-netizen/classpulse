My courses

All the documents of the course, the labs and the tutorials: course notes, lab sheets, code.

First time? Register in a course

You choose a PIN and you can read your documents at once. Your phone is linked later, in class, the first time you scan the QR code of a session. Not in the official list of the course? Register in class, while the teacher opens the attendance.

In class: join a session with its code

In class, scan the QR code shown on the screen: nothing to type. Use the code only if you cannot scan.

📲 Put ClassPulse on your home screen (no more address to remember)

Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ at the top right › Add to Home screen › Add.

iPhone (Safari): the Share button (square with an arrow) › Add to Home Screen › Add.

An icon "ClassPulse" appears with your other apps. Use the same browser as the one you registered with: your phone stays recognised.

During a session, quizzes and demos are sent to your phone by the teacher. Outside the sessions, all the documents stay available in "My documents".
