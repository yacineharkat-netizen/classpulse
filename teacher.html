Registration of the students

A student registers once, from the home page or in class: student number, last name, first name, PIN. He must be in the official list (student number and last name). The phone he registers with becomes his class device (attendance and quizzes); after a registration made on a computer, the first device he uses in a session (phone or computer) becomes his class device. With another device he asks from the session page, for one session or as his new class device: you accept in the Live session tab or here. You can also decide yourself: Allow a new device. Too many wrong PINs: Unlock PIN. Somebody registered in his place, or PIN forgotten: Reset PIN.

Stations

Who is on which station

Several students may share a station (pairs, groups of three). Change a station at any time: no penalty, and the numeric answers already given are marked again with the values of the new station. During the test a student may also ask for another station: you accept or refuse it in the Live tab.

Check of the values used

Each numeric answer that depends on the station is checked with the values of every station. A student is flagged when another station fits his answers clearly better than the station he typed (he changed group, or read the wrong line of the table). Nothing changes until you click Recalculate: his numeric answers are then marked with the station you choose, minus the penalty you choose. The correction can be undone.
