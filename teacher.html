Registration of the students

A student registers once, from the home page or in class: student number, last name, first name, PIN. He must be in the official list (student number and last name). The phone he registers with becomes his class device (attendance and quizzes); after a registration made on a computer, the first device he uses in a session (phone or computer) becomes his class device. With another device he asks from the session page, for one session or as his new class device: you accept in the Live session tab or here. You can also decide yourself: Allow a new device. Too many wrong PINs: Unlock PIN. Somebody registered in his place, or PIN forgotten: Reset PIN.

What the lab assistants of this class see

The assistants of this class (Classes tab) find here the content you give to the students: documents of the student space, your resources (files and links pushed in class) and your demos. They can open it, download it if you allow it, and push it to the phones in the lab sessions they run. Everything they open or download is written in their activity log. They never change or delete anything. Read only removes the download buttons; it cannot stop a photo or a screenshot.

Each line below can differ from the class setting (for example: answers hidden, code downloadable). Each quiz can also differ (Results tab).

Who reads what

My documents (private)

Your documents are stored in the ClassPulse database, not on the public site: nobody can read them without being registered in a class where you made them visible. One document = a page (HTML file), and optionally its PDF and an attached file (code zip…). The same document can be used in several classes.

Question bank

Fill the Excel template (one question per row, up to 5 options A-E, correct answers as letters, e.g. C or A,D). Importing a row with an existing ref replaces the question.

The question bank is shared by all your classes: the module name is what keeps the questions of each course apart. Pick an existing module in the list or type a new one.

Edit the question

Tick the correct option(s). Leave an option empty to remove it (2 to 8 options). A question with no correct option is a survey question (opinions, not graded).

Prepared quizzes

A prepared quiz is built once (questions and settings), then run in as many sessions as you want, in any of your classes. Each run keeps its own answers and marks. Changing a prepared quiz never changes a past run.

Results of the quizzes

Every finished quiz and test of the selected class. Open one to see the success rate of each question and the answers and mark of each student. You can correct a mark (with a reason): the corrected mark replaces the computed one in the profile, the averages and the Excel export. A quiz nobody answered is marked empty: it is left out of the marks. You see the lab quizzes the teacher shares with you.

What the assistants did

Board check

Each numeric answer that depends on the board is checked with the values of every board. A student is flagged when another board fits his answers clearly better than the board he typed (he changed group, or read the wrong line of the table). Nothing changes until you click Recalculate: his numeric answers are then marked with the board you choose, minus the penalty you choose. The correction can be undone, it is shown on his phone (mark), in his profile and in the Excel export.
